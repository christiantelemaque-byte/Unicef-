Who we are

UNICEF works in over 190 countries and territories to protect the rights of every child.

We protect children's rights and build a better future

UNICEF is the United Nations agency responsible for ensuring that every child, especially the most vulnerable and excluded, can survive, grow and reach their full potential. We are present in more than 190 countries and territories, and our work is funded entirely by voluntary contributions.

We provide and promote health, education and nutrition services; protect children from violence and abuse; bring clean water and sanitation to communities in need; and protect people from the effects of climate change and disease.

💉 As the world's largest vaccine provider, UNICEF also runs the largest humanitarian warehouse on the planet. We are on the ground before, during and after emergencies to deliver life‑saving help.

No matter who they are or where they live

Impartial, apolitical and neutral – we focus on helping any child in need. UNICEF was founded after World War II to help children whose lives and futures were at risk, regardless of which side their countries had been on. Our mission has not changed since 1946. Today, UNICEF runs the world's largest humanitarian supply chain.

A global team with committed partners

Day after day, our more than 17,000 employees worldwide work to protect children's rights, prevent harm, ensure their growth, education and opportunities to reach their potential. We never give up. UNICEF has decades of field experience, strong partnerships and an innovative spirit.

We are accountable

All UNICEF personnel have a responsibility to protect the communities we serve. That means no one should be harmed by the projects we support or by people associated with us. UNICEF conducts independent investigations to ensure any violation of our ethical standards or regulations is investigated and sanctioned.

We are also accountable to our donors. Our work is funded entirely by voluntary donations and we transparently publish the source and destination of funds.
